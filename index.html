Our story

Started with one kettle and a folding table.

Chaiholic opened in Model Town in 2019 as a single kulhad-chai counter for students walking home from tuition. It grew into a full cafe — sandwiches, pasta, shakes, pizza — but the chai is still made the same way: full-fat milk, hand-crushed spices, and a kettle that never fully cools down.

We're mid-way through the biggest change since we opened: an online counter, so you can book a table before you leave home, or send us an inquiry for a group order, birthday booking, or franchise question without waiting on hold.
